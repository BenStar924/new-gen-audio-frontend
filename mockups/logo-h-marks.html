Hamilton “H” — mark exploration

Each mark shown large, then at 40px and 24px (the sizes it would actually appear at in the console header), then reversed on brand navy. The small row is the real test — a mark that only works large is not usable here. Navy is #2B2A5E, sampled from the wordmark.

A · SOLID TILE
Safest option. Holds at 24px, reverses cleanly, needs no detail to survive.
B · VAULT DOOR
Bolts and ring nod to safes. The ring and bolts mud together below ~32px.
C · SHIELD
Reads “security” instantly. Also the most generic — every security firm has one.
D · TWO-TONE PILLARS
Free-standing, no container. Crossbar in the lighter navy gives it a second colour.
E · DIAL / LOCK
Combination dial sits in the crossbar. Clever large; the dial closes up at 24px.
F · HEXAGON
Bolt-head geometry, machined feel. Holds small nearly as well as the tile.
G · SLOT
Deposit slot cut through the crossbar. Loses the slot at 24px and reads as a plain H.
H · PLATE
Squared steel plate with an inset rule. Closest to the boxy wordmark.
